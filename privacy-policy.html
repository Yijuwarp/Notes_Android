Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

OmniBox Notes ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using the OmniBox Notes Android application.

1. Information Storage and Privacy Architecture

OmniBox Notes is built with a local-first, privacy-conscious architecture:

  • Local Device Storage: All notes, text clips, and attached images are stored locally on your device within an encrypted internal app directory.
  • Zero Third-Party Tracking: OmniBox Notes does not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any user data, note content, or analytics to third-party advertisers or tracking networks.

2. Google Drive Backup & Sync

If you optionally choose to sign in with your Google Account within OmniBox Notes:

  • AppData Scope Usage: We request strict access to https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.
  • Private Application Storage: Backup data is written strictly to your private, hidden Google Drive App Data folder. OmniBox Notes cannot view, access, or alter any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Permissions Requested

  • Internet Access: Used exclusively for Google Sign-In authentication and syncing backups directly to your personal Google Drive account.
  • System Photo Picker: OmniBox Notes uses Android's native system Photo Picker to attach images without requiring broad storage access permissions.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository at Yijuwarp/Notes_Android.
